Privacy Policy

Effective August 14, 2026

Friendli is a social app for making plans with people you actually want to see. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

What we collect

  • Phone number — used to create and secure your account (verified by a one-time SMS code) and to let friends who already have your number find you. Never shown to other users.
  • Profile — your name, photo, short bio, and interests. Visible to other users per the app's visibility rules.
  • Connections and circles — who you're connected to, and which circle tier you place each person in. Tier assignments are private: no one ever sees which tier you put them in.
  • Contacts (optional) — if you grant access, the names and phone numbers in your address book are sent to our servers once per lookup, solely to show you which contacts are already on Friendli and let you invite the rest. We don't store your address book. We keep only the phone numbers you've explicitly sent an invite to, so the same person is never invited twice.
  • Plans, availability, and messages — plans you create or join, your "Open to Plans" windows, and messages, photos, and videos you send in plan chats. Visible only to the people the app's rules say can see them.
  • Hangout reflections — after a hangout you can privately note how it felt. These reflections, and any scores derived from them, are never shown to anyone — not the people you met, not anyone. They only tune your own recommendations.
  • Push tokens — so we can send notifications you've enabled. You control every category in Settings, and can turn them all off.

What we never do

  • No ads, no selling or renting your data, no data brokers.
  • No posting or contacting anyone on your behalf. Invites are sent from your own Messages app, by you.
  • Silent rejections stay silent — declining a request is never announced to the other person.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, authentication, storage), Twilio (delivery of SMS verification codes), and OneSignal (push notification delivery). Each receives only what's needed to provide its service.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account permanently removes your profile, connections, plans, messages, reflections, and photos. This is immediate and cannot be undone. You can also just email us and we'll do it for you.

Age

Friendli is for adults 18 and up.
